Strategy, management and finance at the frontier of new technology.

Bonneville advises founders, boards and executive teams on strategy, operations and financing: from company building and P&L ownership to fundraising, cross-border co-production and go-to-market.

Led by Fred Volhuer, co-founder and former CEO of Atlas V, whose productions won more than 30 international awards including an Emmy, and Guest Professor at Arizona State University. Sector experience spans emerging technology, immersive and spatial media, robotics, applied AI and brand marketing including social platforms.

01The practice

Where the studio is engaged.

  1. i.

    Strategy & general management

    Board and C-level counsel on positioning, operating model and organisational design, including P&L ownership, scale-up sequencing and the decisions that follow a change of direction.

  2. ii.

    Finance, funding & co-production

    Budget architecture, fundraising and investor readiness, cross-border co-production, tax-credit stacks and public funding pipelines, built on a decade of executive production experience.

  3. iii.

    Growth, marketing & social

    Positioning, audience strategy and go-to-market, including social platform strategy and community- and creator-led distribution for brands and technology products.

  4. iv.

    Emerging technology

    Social and humanoid robotics, immersive and spatial computing, applied AI, framed as a business question rather than a technical one: what to build, what to buy, what to ignore, and what it costs either way.

  5. v.

    Innovation & product strategy

    Turning a technology ambition into a roadmap a business can act on: opportunity framing, platform selection, build-versus-partner decisions and phasing.

  6. vi.

    Public funding & consortia

    Assembling international partners and structuring programme architecture for research grants and public funding, from Horizon Europe and France 2030 to the CNC and NSF.
